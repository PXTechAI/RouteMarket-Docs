Complete these 6 steps in order to go from zero to a working RouteMarket integration.

## Steps

1. [Register an account](/en/getting-started/register)
2. [Log in](/en/getting-started/login)
3. [Purchase quota](/en/getting-started/quota)
4. [Create an API token](/en/getting-started/token)
5. [Check your environment](/en/getting-started/env-check)
6. [Configure your CLI tool](/en/getting-started/cli-setup)

> We recommend reading the [Model Marketplace](/en/routing-and-models/model-marketplace) and the [FAQ](/en/faq2/faq-cc) first — they answer most of the questions that come up during setup.
