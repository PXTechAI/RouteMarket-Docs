---
title: 1. Register an account
description: Create your RouteMarket account.
---

Visit the [console registration page](https://console.routemarket.ai/register) and complete registration using either method:

- Continue with your Google / GitHub / MetaMask account
- Register with an email address and password

<DocImage src="/images/docs/getting-started/register.png" alt="RouteMarket registration page" caption="Registration page screenshot" />

We recommend using an email your team can access long-term, and completing any email verification the page requires.

Once registered, continue to [Log in](/en/getting-started/login).
