Open the "Wallet" or "Billing" page in the console sidebar and complete a top-up.

<DocImage src="/images/docs/getting-started/quota.png" alt="RouteMarket purchase quota page" caption="Placeholder — replace with an actual screenshot of the top-up page." />

## Steps

1. Pick a preset amount, or click "Custom amount" to enter your own.
2. Confirm the "Total due" shown on the page.
3. Click "Pay now" and follow the prompts to complete payment.
